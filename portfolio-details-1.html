Project Overview

The Artalog website is a company profile platform developed to professionally represent PT Artalog Teknologi Indonesia in the digital space. The primary goal of this project was to deliver a modern, responsive, and accessible website that showcases the company’s identity, services, and portfolio, while enhancing trust and engagement with potential clients and business partners. The website also features an integrated contact form that sends user-submitted inquiries directly to the company’s email, improving communication efficiency and accessibility.

Project Objective

  • Strengthen the company’s digital presence and credibility.
  • Present clear and structured information about the company’s services, vision, mission, and team.
  • Facilitate communication with potential clients through an integrated contact form and clear contact information.

My Role

  • Designed the site structure and user flow based on client requirements.
  • Developed user interface and ensured an intuitive user experience (UI/UX).
  • Implemented all front-end and back-end functionalities.
  • Conducted testing, debugging, and performance optimization.
  • Deployed the website to its official domain and ensured basic security measures.

Technologies Used

  • Framework: Laravel (PHP)
  • Frontend: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, Bootstrap
  • Email Integration: Laravel Mail (SMTP)
  • Tools & Deployment:Composer, Laravel Artisan, Web Hosting
